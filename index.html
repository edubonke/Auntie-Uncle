GOOD TO KNOW
A few helpful answers.
Are the helpers on this page real?+
No. The names, experience summaries, rates and locations shown are fictional examples designed to help you try the marketplace experience. No providers have been screened or onboarded on this prototype.
Who sets the prices?+
In the proposed marketplace, each independent helper sets their own rates and agrees on a final quote with the household. Displayed sample rates are purely illustrative.
Does sending a request confirm a booking?+
No. In this prototype, requests can be previewed and copied but are not sent. In the future service, a helper would need to accept, confirm price and availability, and agree to the booking terms first.
Will profiles be verified?+
We plan to build a documented onboarding and screening process with suitable safeguards. No identity, background or qualification checks have been conducted for these fictional profiles. For childcare in particular, additional safety and qualification requirements must be established before launch.
Do helpers work for Auntie & Uncle?+
The planned model is a marketplace for independently operating providers, not direct household-service employment. Actual legal arrangements, obligations and provider terms must be reviewed before launch.
